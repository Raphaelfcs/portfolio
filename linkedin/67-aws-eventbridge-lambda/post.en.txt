Event-driven integration is only reliable when you know what happens to the message that failed.

The pattern I use to integrate accounts and partners on AWS:
→ A dedicated EventBridge bus, opened only to the producer’s roles
→ A rule delivering into an encrypted SQS queue, with DLQs on the rule and the queue
→ An archive for replay without depending on the producer
→ Least-privilege Lambda with X-Ray and partial batch failures
→ Alarms for a stopped consumer, old messages and the DLQ

The code, tests and diagram are on GitHub.
