VPC peering works until the tenth account. After that, the network needs a hub.

The network model I use in multi-account AWS environments:
→ A Network account with a Transit Gateway shared through RAM
→ Segmented route tables: prod, nonprod and shared
→ Direct Connect over a transit VIF and a Site-to-Site VPN with BGP as backup
→ Three-tier VPCs, data with no internet route, and VPC endpoints to cut NAT cost

It is the same model I used to integrate a partner’s network without exposing the other VPCs.
