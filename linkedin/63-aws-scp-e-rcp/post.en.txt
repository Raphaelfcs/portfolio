An SCP alone does not stop an outside account from reading your bucket. An RCP alone does not stop someone inside from turning off CloudTrail.

What I apply in the multi-account landing zones I build:
→ SCPs on the OUs: allowed regions, protected CloudTrail/GuardDuty/Config/Security Hub, root denied, encryption and tags on create
→ Data-perimeter RCPs: S3, KMS, SQS, STS and Secrets Manager only accept identities from the organization and declared accounts, over TLS
→ A budget with a freeze SCP when spend blows past the limit

All in Terraform, with tests that check the policy content before apply.
