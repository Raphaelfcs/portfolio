Challenge: store exams, diagnoses and prescriptions as PDFs for 20 clinics today and 3,000 tomorrow, with each patient opening their own in one click. Security first, AWS and Google Cloud, and a US$ 200 a month ceiling.

The solution I designed and coded:
→ Active-active on both clouds: Route 53 splits the traffic and pulls the failing cloud out of DNS in about a minute
→ Serverless and event-driven: Lambda and Cloud Run scale from zero, and a PDF’s arrival triggers validation and replication to the other cloud
→ PDFs go straight to the bucket through signed URLs and are validated before reaching the patient
→ Per-clinic and per-patient isolation covered by tests, and patients see who accessed their documents (LGPD)
→ No stored keys between clouds: OIDC and Workload Identity Federation
→ Data in São Paulo, customer-managed keys on both clouds and 20-year retention

Estimated cost: US$ 48 a month with 20 clinics and US$ 75 with 100. At 3,000, sign-in becomes 85% of the bill, and the article shows the ways out.

Modular Terraform (6 roots with dev, staging and prod, 14 new modules with tests), a Python API and a web app you can install on your phone.
